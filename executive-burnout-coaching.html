Succeeding on the outside. Running on empty inside.

You don't have a discipline problem

You've optimised everything. You're still bone-tired.

You have read the books. You have the planner, the morning routine, the 7am training block, maybe the assistant and the cold plunge. And you are still running on fumes by Wednesday. The problem was never effort. You are exhausted because your body has been in fight-or-flight for years, and no amount of optimisation regulates a dysregulated system.

You are not weak, lazy, or failing. You are running the wrong operating system. High performers do not burn out because they are fragile. They burn out because they are good at overriding the signals, right up until the body stops asking nicely. The fix is not more willpower. It is fixing the foundation the performance is built on.

How it shows up

If two of these are you, we should talk.

Wired but tired

You are shattered and you still cannot switch off. The mind races at 2am. Rest feels like another thing you are failing at.

The edge is dulling

The sharpness that got you here is slipping. Slower decisions, shorter fuse, the work taking more out of you for less back.

Sleep doesn't fix it

You sleep and wake up tired anyway. This is not a sleep-hours problem. It is a nervous system that never came down.

Performing while empty

You are delivering and hiding how depleted you are. The model that built your success, push harder, extract more, has stopped paying off.

The method

Fix the system first. The R.A.C.E. Framework.

Recovery-led coaching runs on a four-phase method, the same one in the book and behind The Unbroken Protocol, applied to the way you actually work.

Recover — down-regulate the nervous system. Sleep, HRV, and a real off-switch, so your body stops treating Tuesday like a threat.
Align — match the demands to your true capacity, not an imaginary one. Performance that fits the life and the load you actually carry.
Condition — train the system to handle pressure, instead of grinding through it until it breaks.
Execute — simple systems that hold when the week gets messy, so you perform without paying for it in health.

It is the opposite of extraction-based performance. You do not perform your way to recovery. You recover your way to performance.

Questions

Before you reach out.

If I slow down, won't I lose my edge?

No, and this is the fear that keeps high performers stuck. Your edge is dulling because you are running on a depleted system. A regulated nervous system is sharper: better decisions, faster recovery between demands, energy that lasts. You recover your way back to your edge.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Coaching, not therapy. We work with your physiology, your systems, and your performance. If you need clinical support, Abraham will say so.

How does this fit a packed schedule?

It is built for one. Recovery-led coaching is the minimum effective dose, the few things that move your physiology, fitted around real demands, not another to-do list.

What does it cost?

The first conversation is a free coffee, in London or online. Coaching runs in three monthly tiers; Abraham recommends the right level after, no pitch.
