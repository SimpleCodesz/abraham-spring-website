## Technical diagnosis

The previous Netlify catch-all served `index.html` with status 200 for every unknown path. GSC performance data showed obsolete URLs such as `/store`, `/contact`, `/about-us` and `/blog-1-1/sleep` still receiving impressions. This campaign removes the catch-all, installs relevant 301s for those four URLs and restores a true 404 boundary through the existing `404.html`.

The SE Ranking production crawl completed against 23 live pages with a 95% score: 114 checks passed, 3 errors and 54 notices. The three errors were two `noindex` legal pages included in the XML sitemap and one orphaned podcast page. This campaign removes the legal pages from the sitemap and adds a homepage link to the podcast. The remaining prominent notices—12 long titles and 19 long descriptions—are being reduced first on the six ownership pages rather than mechanically rewriting every page without regard to intent.
