A few things to know.

Is this ongoing coaching?

This is a defined consultancy package with preparation, two appointments, a personal map and one clarification email. Daily messaging and ongoing programme management are outside this package.

Do I need WHOOP or another wearable?

No. Your experience and routines are the starting point. If you use a wearable, we can consider its trends alongside that context.

What does the personal map look like?

A concise summary of your main priorities, the actions to practise, how they fit your week and what to observe before our follow-up.

Does this replace medical care or therapy?

No. This is performance, recovery and coaching guidance. It does not diagnose or treat medical or mental health conditions. Where an issue needs another professional, we’ll recognise that boundary.

How do I book?

Start with an enquiry. We’ll confirm suitability, availability, payment arrangements and app access before you commit. Please keep the initial form to your general goal and contact details.
