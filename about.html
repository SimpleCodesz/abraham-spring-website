About Abraham

I have burnt out chasing performance.
So I built the way out.

I am a father of four. I pushed harder, did more and powered through, until it cost me my health and my sleep. Coming out of it, I went looking for what I had missed. That search became a method, a book and a company.

Now I work one to one with people who cannot afford to break themselves to keep going.
