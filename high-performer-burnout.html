High-performer burnout coaching · London and online

Burnout coaching for high performers who cannot switch off.

From the outside you are winning. Inside, you are running on fumes and hiding it, because somewhere along the way you became what you produce, and now rest feels like losing yourself. I help high performers find the self underneath the output, so recovery stops feeling like failure and starts being the thing that keeps you in the game.

Have a coffee with Abraham › How it works

It's not weakness

When you are what you produce, rest feels like disappearing.

The trap is quiet and it is built into how you got here. Your worth got fused to your output. So when your body asks for rest, every instinct reads it as a threat, slow down and you might lose your edge, your status, your sense of who you are. So you override it. Again and again, until override stops working.

That is not a character flaw. It is a nervous system that learned to equate stillness with danger, and a high achiever who got very good at ignoring it. The way out is not to care less about performance. It is to build a foundation underneath it, so you can recover without feeling like you are vanishing, and perform from fullness instead of fear.

How it shows up

If two of these are you, we should talk.

Rest feels like failure

A day off comes with guilt, not relief. Stillness makes you anxious, so you fill it, and you never actually recover.

Winning, joylessly

You hit the goal and feel nothing, just the next target. The wins stopped landing a while ago and you have not let yourself notice.

Hiding the empty

Everyone thinks you are thriving. You are managing the gap between how you look and how you feel, and that gap is its own full-time job.

The model stopped working

Push harder, extract more, the formula that built your success is now the thing draining it, and you do not have another one.

The method

Perform from fullness. The R.A.C.E. Framework.

The four-phase method from the book and behind The Unbroken Protocol, applied to a life built on output.

Recover — teach the nervous system that stillness is safe again. Sleep, HRV, and real rest, without the guilt.
Align — reconnect performance to something underneath it, so your worth is not riding on the next result.
Condition — train capacity, not just output, so you can carry load without it carrying you.
Execute — perform from a full tank, with systems that do not depend on you running on empty.

Extraction is why you are breaking. The answer was never more. You do not perform your way to recovery. You recover your way to performance, and to a version of it you can actually live inside.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Why does slowing down feel like failing?

Because your worth got fused to your output. If you are what you produce, rest feels like disappearing. The work is to build a self underneath the performance, so recovery stops feeling like loss.

I'm still delivering. Am I really burnt out?

Output does not always collapse first. You can keep hitting the numbers while the internal cost rises and recovery gives less back. The evidence-reviewed guide am I burnt out—or just tired? explains the work-related pattern, its limits and when clinical support matters.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Coaching, not therapy. We work with your physiology, your identity around performance, and your systems. If you need clinical support, Abraham will say so.

What does it cost?

The first conversation is a free coffee, in London or online. Coaching runs in three monthly tiers; Abraham recommends the right level after, no pitch.
