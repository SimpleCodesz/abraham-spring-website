The method

R.A.C.E.

Recover. Align. Condition. Execute. Start with the nervous system and build outward, so the plan holds under pressure.

Read the method

R

Recover

Stop training on a broken battery. Sleep, breath and recovery come before anything else.

A

Align

Filter your ambition through your reality. Training that fits your life, not the other way round.

C

Condition

Find the smallest dose that gets the result. More is not better. Better is better.

E

Execute

Build a plan that survives a bad day. Simple systems outlast willpower.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this coaching or consultancy?

Consultancy. I read your data and your life together, then build and maintain a plan with you. It is performance and recovery guidance, not medical advice or treatment.

Which wearables does it work with?

Most of them. If your device feeds Apple Health or Strava, or you can export your data, we can work with it. Bring what you have.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. Athletes, parents, founders and executives all carry load. If you train, or want to, and want it to last, this is for you.

Is it online or in person?

Both. I am based in London and work with people online and in person.

How do the app, book and club fit in?

They are the system around the consultancy. The app holds your plan and daily signals, the book explains the method, and the club gives you people to train with.

I'm a parent with no time. Will this actually work?

Yes. The whole system is built around the constraint, not against it. Abraham is a father of four. Training that doesn't survive school runs, sleepless nights, and a real job is not training, it's a wish list.

I've got a Garmin and a WHOOP. Can you tell me which is right?

Often, yes. Different devices measure different proxies for the same nervous system. We can read them together and find the signal in the noise. The point is clarity, not more data.

What is the R.A.C.E. Framework?

R.A.C.E. stands for Recover, Align, Condition, Execute. A trademarked, published, four-phase methodology that starts with nervous system regulation and builds outward. The alternative to extraction-based coaching.
