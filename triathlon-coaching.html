Triathlon

Triathlon that starts with recovery, not volume.

Three disciplines, one nervous system. Most triathletes break because they keep adding hours to a body already in the red. Recovery-led coaching trains you from where your system actually is each morning, so the swim, bike and run compound instead of grinding you into the same wall every season.

The difference

You don't adapt to training. You adapt to training you've recovered from.

Volume-led coaching assumes more is more. But a triathlete is juggling three sports, a job, and a life, and the load lands on one shared nervous system. Pile on hours without recovery and the engine keeps improving on paper while the foundation quietly fails, until an injury, illness, or burnout crash resets you to zero. Again.

Recovery-led coaching reads your nervous system each morning, through HRV, sleep, and an honest check-in, and matches the day to it. Hard when you can take it, easy when you cannot, and the discipline to know the difference. The result is consistency that compounds: fewer lost weeks, fewer breakdowns, and a body that is still building in week twelve instead of falling apart.

Questions

Triathlon, answered.

What makes recovery-led coaching different?

Most coaching is volume-led: more hours, push through. Recovery-led starts each day with where your nervous system actually is and matches the training to it, so the work compounds instead of grinding you down.

Where's the full system, app and club?

At The Unbroken Protocol: the app with the daily Protocol Score, the swim/bike/run/strength craft, and the Unbroken Triathlon Club. This page is the human coaching layer.

Can it work alongside my current plan?

Yes. R.A.C.E. sits underneath whatever you are on. Some run it alongside their coach or app; others switch. We find the fit over coffee.
